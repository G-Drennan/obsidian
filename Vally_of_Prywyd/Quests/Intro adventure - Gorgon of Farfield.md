The players start in the small town of Farfield,  a small remote farming village.
People have been going missing in the woods, a search party found the statue of a young hunter known by the village. The village has since smelted down their available metal to create pikes to surround the village in to protect it.

- By the entrance to a cave is a ward that has been damaged.
- Its raining.

## Adventure
**Background:** An elven priest welcomed a group of adventures into her cave when a storm hit but told them to not go into her room or the ritual chamber. Discovering her a practitioner of magic when she refused their gifts of wine laced with iron -as is customary- they attacked her. She felt to the ritual chamber begging her ancestors to allow her to pass into the magic realm only for the adventures to smash the mirror to prevent her from summoning demons. This cursed her and turned the adventures to Jade.
### Role Play challenge
Players are here to find out why the crops haven't been delivered.
Have a players relation in the village be missing.
- Rumours of talking stone. “The stone is remembering things it shouldn’t.”
- Village keeps the events happening in the wood secret as to avoid people believing the village is cursed. "No one would buy grain from a cursed village."
- Farmers have stopped working the fields, only half the crop has been harvested.
	- "The forest is to close to the field, farmers who strayed too far haven't returned"
- A hunters who has been known for their attendance of the inn every night has not visited in a while, the bar tender will ask the players check in them. 
	- The hunter watched her companion turn to stone as they were hunting near the cursed cave. "The symbols around the cave show that its a cursed place."
	- The hunter doesn't use her right hand which is gloved while her left hand is ungloved. This is because she was attacked by the gorgon and barely survived.  
- The blacksmiths daughter will stalk the player through the town when she is caught she will beg the PCs leave, "All this talk of the cursed forest has increased the demand of my farther work. Please leave well enough alone."
#### Tavern
**Adversaries:** Guards, Mercenaries. 
**Impulses:** Provide opportunities for adventures, nurture community. 
##### Features

- **Rumours - Passive:** Roll *Presences* as 1 on a success 2 on a critical success.
	- A fascinating rumour with a connection to the PC backstory.
	- A promising job for the party involving a nearby threat or situation.
	- Local folklore that relates to something they've seen.
	- Town gossip that hints at a community problem.
	*Who has the information? What gossip do locals start spreading about the PC?*
- **Sing for your supper- Passive:**  Roll *Presence*, earn 1d4 gold on a success 2d4 on a critical. Mark stress on a failure. 
	*What do you perform? What does the piece mean to you? When was the last time you performed for a crowd?*
- **Bar Fight - Action:** ***Spend a fear*** to have a bar fight erupt in the tavern. when a PC tries to move through the tavern while the fight persists, they must make an *Agility* or *Presence* roll or take 1d6+2 physical damage from a wild swing or thrown object. 
		*Who started the fight? What will it take to stop it?*
	- Fight could be over the blacksmiths prices which have increased as of late due to their work being occupied by making pikes to ward away the cursed forest. This is the last time the blacksmith is drinking in the tavern.

Room full of statues is full of whispers, hinting at a past of magic and how the empire conquered it with metal. 

#### Gorgon [[Cave]] environment
**Difficulty:** Tier 1 
**Impulse:** Shelter, To erode resolve through sensory dread 
**Adversaries:** Gorgon,  Statues, Cave Fauna. 

**Features:**

- **Whispers of the Damned** — _Spend a Fear._ A character rolls a d6; if the result is under their Hope, they lose 1 Hope and gain 1 Stress as the statues murmur. _“A voice you shouldn’t recognize leans close and tells you something you wish you hadn’t heard.”_ _What did you hear that caused you stress?_
    
- **Darkness** — Torchlight is required to see; each torch lasts **2d12 actions**. A cold wind surges unpredictably through the cavern. _“The wind snuffs the flame for a heartbeat, and the dark presses in like a living thing.”_
    
- **Flood** — _Costs Fear._ Heavy rain pours through blocked gutters of debris and abandoned gear. The chamber floods in **2d12 actions**. _“Water rises around your ankles, carrying the smell of old stone and forgotten travelers.”_
### Climax
Fight the gorgon who is trying to repair a broken mirror that doesn't reflect images, only a turning mist.  The gorgon was cursed after a group of adventures took shelter in the cave, ignoring the wards placed outside the cave. these adventures were her first victims and are why she doesn't trust anyone who enters her cave anymore. She was once the priest of this shrine.
- When a player is turned to stone ask: '**What part of your identity others have misjudged because of your appearance ?**'
- Light will blind the gorgon. 
- Killing her or repairing the mirror will remove the curse. 
	- fixing the mirror will require the party goes to the next shrine, travel will take a session and engaging the priest for help will be another session. 
- Diary will tell her story if the players kill her. 
- Add in the gorgon animating 2-3 of the statues that where the adventures or hunter.
