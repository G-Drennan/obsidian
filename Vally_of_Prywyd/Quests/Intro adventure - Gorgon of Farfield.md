The players start in the small town of Farfield,  a small remote farming village. They are here to find out why crops and lumber have stopped being delivered for **[[Lord Theodore Hardwyn]]**. 

People have been going missing in the woods, a search party found the statue of a young Lumberjack known by the village. The village has since smelted down their available metal to create pikes to surround the village in to protect it. *Have a players relation in the village be missing.*
## Adventure
**Background:** An elven priest welcomed a group of adventures into her cave when a storm hit but told them to not go into her room or the ritual chamber. Discovering her a practitioner of magic when she refused their gifts of wine laced with iron -as is customary- they attacked her. She felt to the ritual chamber begging her ancestors to allow her to pass into the magic realm only for the adventures to smash the mirror to prevent her from summoning demons. This cursed her and turned the adventures to Jade.
### Farfield village: Roleplay challenge

- Rumors of talking stone. “The stone is remembering things it shouldn’t.”
- Village keeps the events happening in the wood secret as to avoid people believing the village is cursed. "No one would buy grain from a cursed village or lumber from a cursed forest."
- Farmers have stopped working the fields, only half the crop has been harvested.
	- "The forest is to close to the field, farmers who strayed too far haven't returned"

- A hunters who has been known for their attendance of the inn every night has not visited in a while, the bar tender will ask the players check in them. She is sulking in the hunters guild which is empty now the forest is believed cursed
	- The hunter watched her companion turn to stone as they were hunting near the cursed cave. "The symbols around the cave show that its a cursed place."
	- The hunter doesn't use her right hand which is gloved while her left hand is ungloved. This is because she was attacked by the gorgon and barely survived.  
	- "If you find out what happened to my companion I will reward you with the hunters guild crest, it will allow you to legally hunt and sell game"

Please leave well enough alone and go back to the bigger towns where you will surely find better paying work."

### Entrance & Guardian
*The entrance to the cave is steep. Its wards have been strike through. History says this is likely the work of the empire who despised magic.*
- A humanoid jade statue, covered in mud is hidden in the bushes not far from the cave entrance. This is the missing hunter. 
- The gutter that lines this chamber is clogged and water from the rain outside leads down further into the cave. 
- There is a secret door that hides the entrance to the Priest living chambers. The chamber is hinted at by the presence of a gutter by the door to prevent the cave from flooding.
	- A homely warm room furnished with hand crafted wooden furniture, a well stocked panty, a desk and small bookshelf, and a hay bed.

To the side of chamber space is a spring sounded by large stones to collect the water. If someone lived here it was for much longer than the camp that's set up in the chamber.*

#### Gorgon [[Cave]] environment
**Adversaries:** [[DH-SRD-May202025.pdf#page=87&selection=0,0,0,6|Gorgon]],  Statues ([[DH-SRD-May202025.pdf#page=80&selection=192,0,192,9|Sellsword]]). 

**Features:**
- **Whispers of the Damned** — _Spend a Fear._ A character rolls a d6; if the result is under their Hope, they lose 1 Hope and gain 1 Stress as the statues murmur. _“A voice you shouldn’t recognize leans close and tells you something you wish you hadn’t heard.”_ _What did you hear that caused you stress?_
- **Darkness** — Torchlight is required to see; each torch lasts **2d12 actions**. A cold wind surges unpredictably through the cavern. _“The wind snuffs the flame for a heartbeat, and the dark presses in like a living thing.”_
- **Flood** — _Costs Fear._ Heavy rain pours through blocked gutters of debris and abandoned gear. The chamber floods in **2d12 actions**. _“Water rises around your ankles, carrying the smell of old stone and forgotten travelers.”_
### Climax
*The room is lush with vegetation and moister. Jade chunks litter the floor. At the back of the space is an alter with a shattered mirror behind it, the image within it missing. *
- Fight the gorgon who is trying to repair a broken mirror that doesn't reflect images, only a turning mist and shifting shadows.  
- When a player is turned to Jade ask: '**What part of your identity others have misjudged because of your appearance ?**'
- Light will blind the gorgon. 
- Killing her or repairing the mirror will remove the curse. 
	- fixing the mirror will require the party goes to the next shrine, travel will take a session and engaging the priest for help will be another session. 
- Diary will tell her story if the players kill her. 
- Add in the gorgon animating 2-3 of the statues that where the adventures or hunter. 
- Smashed jade can be found in the room, a keen eye with recognizes that they have detailed humanoid features as well as the war hammer that resting against a wall.
