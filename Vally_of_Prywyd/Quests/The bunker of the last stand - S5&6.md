## Background
The last time this dungeon was designed as a bunker/refuge in case of the lich returning. In actuality the mage class held the knowledge of its existence secret and intended it to be a place for them alone to use in case of an cataclysmic event. 

A sect of maintainers keep the bunker in working order and occasionally let people of the mage class use it as a place to lay low, turning it from a practical space to one of comfort and luxury. 

It was also used as a secret meeting place by the mage class - place for them to convene away from the central seat of their power. 

The rebellion against the mage class drove them to this location intentionally before attacking them systematically. the rebellion smashed the mirrors in the space to prevent the mage lords from fleeing. The ones that survived fled into the [[Aethelgard - The lost Realm|Aethel]] realm, others locked themselves in safe rooms only for them to be locked in by the rebellion. 

Due to the bunker being closed for so long the inside are well maintained, but the growing power of [[the chained lord]] demon now infest the dungeon.
