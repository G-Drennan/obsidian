[[River snakes Bandits]]
## Scene 1 
Bandits are pulling a young woman onto the street claiming her farther has failed to pay his gambling debts and she must pay instead or he dies tonight.
- One of the bandits recognises the party from the bridge. 
- Find map on bandit body or interrogate one.
- A note from a journey page complaining about Joves Riddles to get into the bandits hideout
	- "I know they have to be relatively easy to let our customers in, but I think its a weakness in our security. I should mention this to The Golden Snake."

## Scene 2
Finding the entrance, disable traps, hidden behind water fall and tricking the door man by answering his riddle. 
- "If you seek entry, you gotta use the right tool. It's a hard rod that goes straight into a dark n greasy hole, where it twist and makes the whole thing pop open. What am I talking about?" - Key. 
- Wards that trigger an earth quake that make the bandit cave unstable, they have used supports to prevent the cave from caving in. The increased activity in the caves is awakening the wards.
	- Countdown d6 to cave in. 

## Scene 3
Bandit bar. 
- An alcohol that makes a person breath fire.
- 3 Minions, 2 sulk, and a bandit leader. 
- **Spend a hope to use the environment.** Gain or Reduce +d6 dmg
	- Chandelier to swing from.
	- Barrel of ale to explode.
	- Table to flip.
	- Pot to Shield with.

## Scene 4
Bandit prison, Place indebted farther here, 
- one bruiser guard with torturing equipment  as his weapons with an emblem from a corrupt [[Drakona]] house on them.
	- This Drakona house was ostracised after they broke the Drakona rule on bring foreigners into their country.
	- Symbol of a shooting star. with dragon like wings. 

## Scene 5
Fighting pits and Leader of the bandits tried to bribe the adventures to leave his group alone.
- Bandits try to protect their horde of treasure that is the betting pool for the dog fights.
- **Drakes fight Pit - Passive:** **Spend a fear** to have a player be thrown into the world pit. On a failed check to escape they take 2d8+4 damage from the Drakes. Minions thrown into here automatically are eaten by the hungry Drakes.  
- **Pit Crowd - Passive** The players can gain advantage by convincing the audience to cheer for them. 
- Leader is a [[Drakona]]
