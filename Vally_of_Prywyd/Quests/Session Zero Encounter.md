
**[[Lord Theodore Hardwyn]]:** a owner of the land that Farfield resides on. 

### Background
The party met the lord in a tavern outside the main city, he wanted to avoid increasing the gossip around his missing shipment from Farfield after his last shipment returned with no stock and his ship crew refused to return to the village signing a curse in the forest they wont speak about.  
### Encounter
The druid attacks the players who are riding a wagon with the symbol of their patron, a lord who's family traces their heritage back to military victories against the people of Prywyd valley.
