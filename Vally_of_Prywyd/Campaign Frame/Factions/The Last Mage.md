## Plan
- To restore the valley and free it from empire rule the Last Mage is trying to make contact with a far off kingdom to organise a war to wear down the empire. 
- Their will seek to re-open the [[Aethelgard - The lost Realm|Aethel]] Realmand restore the valley to the power it once had. To do this they must team up with the only person who still has access to magic not hindered by the closing of the [[Aethelgard - The lost Realm|Aethel]] Realm, [[The Lich - The chain lord.]].
