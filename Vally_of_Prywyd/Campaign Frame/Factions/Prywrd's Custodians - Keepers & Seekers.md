A leaderless faction of druids, elves, and magic users who quietly reject the empires rule and seek to maintain the culture of the past. 
### Keepers
- Protect sites of historic significant. 
- They often worship the old god, these god where once people who made significant developments in magic - these development are seen now are divine provenance.
- Keepers often bring in children with magical gifts to teach them the ways of the faction. 

### Seekers
- Find lost knowledge, often dungeon delvers or diplomates that negotiate with the empire's representatives to maintain sacred sites. This is the more forwards facing part of the Sect.
- Some think this sect of the faction to be traitorous due to their relationship with the empire, those that follow this sect believe that progress in preserving their ways can't be made without working with the empire. 

### Wardens
- A sect of the Custodians who are actively fighting with the empire using gorilla tactics.
- They use the same symbol as the main sect but with black ink instead of white ink to represent the taint allowing the empire to remain int he valley has caused to them.
#### Sigrid Soarcrest - Leader of the Wardens
- Giant, with 3 eyes, warpaint, long raven black hair, black leathers, and a green cloak.
- Her village, [[Yindr]], was one of the closest to the empire and often experienced conflict over the years, being one of the hardest hit by the empires war to take over the valley. 
	- When she lived their as a child the village was punished by the empire when they refused to accept raised taxes, the empire instead of raising their taxes claimed the mines the village worked for themselves.
- Sigrid for her time in the custodians as a keeper researched the how the valley fell, this leads her to seek the mysterious [[The Last Mage]], a mythic villain who cursed the valleys unique magic to be weak to metal. If this mage destroyed a magical kingdom, surely knowing how they did it might help remove the empire once and for all. 
- Sigrid gained a reputation of being a strong speaker and leader and was pushed into being a diplomate for the Seekers. It was here she saw the distain the empire still held for the custodians, it was a matter of pleasantries and no real action by the empire. 
	- Her seeker group who were tasked with documenting a newly found ruin of magical significant. They left to get resources thinking no-one would disturbed their site, they only left a handful of young acolytes to guard the site. A roaming troop of [[The Empire|inquisitors]] defiled the site and left the young acolytes dead with a sign warning others of the use of magic. 
	  When she angrily broached this with the courts representative of the custodians, they said nothing could be done for the inquisitors are beyond law. From that day she vowed to remove the empire from the valley.
