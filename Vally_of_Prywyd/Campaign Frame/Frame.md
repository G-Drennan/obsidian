## Overview
The campaign takes place in the **Valley of Prywyd**, a once‑magical kingdom conquered generations ago by the expansionist **Empire of Kority**. The empire’s steel, industry, and renaissance‑era technology have reshaped the valley’s culture, landscape, and identity. Battlefields from the conquest still scar the valley’s natural beauty, and imperial architecture rises where sacred groves once stood.

Magic, once woven into daily life, has faded into myth. Only the **silent Elven priests** who guard distant shrines remember the old ways — and even they are dwindling.

## History
### The Chained Tyrant
 [[The Lich - The chain lord.]], who was once the tyrant of the Valley, is chained by a group of mage heroes who go onto Rule and unite the now splintered Valley of PryWyd.
### Period of excess - The Era of Aethelgard
Magic wielding elves ruled over the valley, and the population grew disenfranchised as their freedoms go taken away all to enforce the power of the mage class. Additionally the reason the mage class had their power in the first place was forgotten as the mages grew comfortable in their own image and power they believed they didn't need to rely on the story they they were the only people maintaining the leash on [[The Lich - The chain lord.]], and the story faded into history. 

A rebel group lead by [[The Last Mage]] over threw the ruling mages by closing the connection to the [[Aethelgard - The lost Realm|Aethel]] Realm Now magics connection to the physical realm could be disrupted by metal. 

The mage class was exiled to the magical realm, and the remaining elves stayed behind to watch over the physical entrances to the magical realm. Building a culture around their protection despite losing why they need to be protected over time.

**Legends from this time** 
- Many of the mage Houses -each having its own guarded specialist magic-  from this period became remembered as gods -their domain reflecting their speciality. The Houses are personified by the mythic story of how the original group of mages -now named by the mage house that claimed to be descendent from them- defeated the lich. 
- The legacy oft he last mage is consider one of societal collapse caused by a rebellious god

### Period of quiet
Humans and other cultures move into the land, now welcome by the remaining elves to rebuild the valley. The valley became groups of farming villages who valued community and mutual aid, the remains of their governing structure still persist in the valleys guild for farmers, militia, blacksmiths, ect. These communities respected the forest mages, now part of the [[Prywrd's Custodians - Keepers & Seekers| Custodian Keepers]], who would bless crops, adjudicate official agreements including marriages and feuds, and provided aid to the sick. 
[[The last mage]] seeing their was no more use for them slept. 

**Beliefs of the valley**
- A common phrase is, 'May the Lynx not follow your steps'
- Mirrors are revered by the valley people as places of passage and transition. They feature often in funerals and births. A Mirror like stone is created in the lakes of Prywyd that lack metal in its composition. 
- During a bad plague Communities that were highly infected were burnt down by the keepers, People now talk of sickness -particularly inflammation symptoms- as a consuming flame and consider it unsafe for sick people to be near flammable objects.
	- This time also cemented burning the dead the the culture of the valley. 
### Modernity
The war between [[The Empire of Ru'Celious]] and the unprepared Valley awoke [[The Last Mage]], as they travel the valley conquered by the empire they see only the faces of the old mage class suppressing and abusing the population for their own profit. What's worse is that the metal that was once the last mage's people savior is now its oppressor, people who show signs of magic are now chained to control them. Distraught the last mage watched on in horror, taking their time with planning how to restore the valley.


**Beliefs imported from the empire** 
- Metal charms prevent magic from being used on you. 
- Bodies destroyed after death can not persist in the after life. 
	- Prevents medical advancements. 
	- Metal is used to preserve bodies. 
## Themes
- **Campaign question:** what is the importance of the past? How can you accept the past? What is the cost of forgiveness? Should traditions be upheld or challenged to change? 
- Accessing raw magic is dangerous. Accessing magic via an intermediary e.g music, nature or a third party, is safer. 
	- [[The Last Mage]] used metal to limit the amount of power that could be accessed this way and closed the most direct ways of accessing it safely and at powerful levels sealing the [[Aethelgard - The lost Realm|Aethel]] Realm. This seal has been weakening but not fast enough for the last mage.
	- Magic that's cast without the limitation of metal can warp the user and have unexpected outcomes.

### Classes
- Druid: People who are part of a secret sect that try to recover lost knowledge of the magic of the valley.
- Sorcerer: People who are born where the lines between the physical realm and the arcane realm are weakest. They often have an innate repulsion to metal, touching it drains their energy and personality most commonly.
- Warlocks (Wizards): Make deals with spirits they contact in places where the veil is thin, this connection often leaves the warlocks appearance warped as an effect of the magic. They are often used by the imperials to give a bad name to spell casters. Warlocks react violently to metal, those who want to live in modernity must wrap themselves and hide marks that signify them as warlocks. 
- Seraph: Commonly imperial aligned holy crusaders or members of the secret sect that try to recover lost knowledge of the magic of the valley. 
- Ranger beast master: Bond spirits from the veil to beast to control them, or so rumours say.
- Bards/Artificer: an ancient art form recovered by the empire. They use instruments to manipulate the arcane realm.
