## Overview
The campaign takes place in the **Valley of Prywyd**, a once‑magical kingdom conquered generations ago by the expansionist **Empire of Kority**. The empire’s steel, industry, and renaissance‑era technology have reshaped the valley’s culture, landscape, and identity. Battlefields from the conquest still scar the valley’s natural beauty, and imperial architecture rises where sacred groves once stood.

Magic, once woven into daily life, has faded into myth. Only the **silent Elven priests** who guard distant shrines remember the old ways — and even they are dwindling.

## History
### The Chained Tyrant
The Litch, who was once the tyrant of the Valley, is chained by a group of mage heroes who go onto Rule and unite the now splintered Valley of PryWyd.
### Period of excess
Magic wielding elves ruled over the valley, and the population grew disenfranchised as their freedoms go taken away all to enforce the power of the mage class. Additionally the reason the mage class had their power in the first place was forgotten as the mages grew comfortable in their own image and power they believed they didn't need to rely on the story they they were the only people maintaining the leash on the Litch, and the story faded into history. 
A rebel group lead by the last mage over threw the ruling mages by closing the connection to the magic realm. Now magics connection to the physical realm could be disrupted by metal. 

The mage class was exiled to the magical realm, and the remaining elves stayed behind to watch over the physical entrances to the magical realm. Building a culture around their protection despite losing why they need to be protected over time.

### Period of quiet
Humans and other cultures move into the land, now welcome by the remaining elves to rebuild the valley. The valley became groups of farming villages who valued community and mutual aid, the remains of their governing structure still persist in the valleys guild for farmers, militia, blacksmiths, ect. 

The last mage seeing their was no more use for them slept

### Modernity
The war between the Empire and the unprepared Valley awoke the last mage, as they travel the valley conquered by the empire they see only the faces of the old mage class suppressing and abusing the population for their own profit. Whats worse is that the metal that was once  the last mage's people saviour is now its oppressor, people who show signs of magic are now chained to control them.  

To solve this the last mage made contact with a far off kingdom to organise a war to wear down the empire while they seek to re-open the magic realm and restore the valley to the power it once had. To do this they must team up with the only person who still has access to magic not hindered by the closing of the magic realm, the Litch. 

## Themes
- **Campaign question:** what is the importance of the past? How can you accept the past? What is the cost of forgiveness? Should traditions be upheld or challenged to change? 
### Ancestry
- Auxiliary (Clank): War machines created by the empire capable of outlasting and out maneuverings an army in a forced march. Recently a revolution of the clanks started in the capital of the empire, Auxiliaries in the valley are considered deserters by the imperials and betrayers of the cause by revolutionaries. 
- Humans: Originate from the empire, The Free Isles, and Halwyd.   
- Dwarf: Seafaring nomads ever since their island home was destroyed by a volcano thousands of years ago, Dwarves are superstitious of land convinced that it lies about its stability, at least the ocean you can tell when it changes. 
- Elves: Keepers of the old magic at shrines, they form small communities that travel from shrine to shrine holding onto their fading culture. City elves have forsaken this life and moved on to modernity.
- Giant: Mountain people. 
- Drakona: A ridged society with a strict hierarchy, before the empire came into conflict with them they were rumored about by dwarf sailors who traded with them.   
- Infernal: The amount of people born as inferals has been increasing over the years, people attribute it to many problems, from abandoned children, the disapproval of the gods, and the practice of magic. Infernals are unique in thier ability to cast magic when in the presence of metal. The empire sends out inquisitors to each village to claim infernal children for unknown purposes.
  The reason for the increasing appearance of infernals is that the lich lord is returning and his magic is reaching out to claim the population as his own.  
### Classes
- Druid: People who are part of a secret sect that try to recover lost knowledge of the magic of the valley.
- Sorcerer: People who are born where the lines between the physical realm and the arcane realm are weakest. They often have an innate repulsion to metal, touching it drains their energy and personality most commonly.
- Warlocks (Wizards): Make deals with spirits they contact in places where the veil is thin, this connection often leaves the warlocks appearance warped as an effect of the magic. They are often used by the imperials to give a bad name to spell casters. Warlocks react violently to metal, those who want to live in modernity must wrap themselves and hide marks that signify them as warlocks. 
- Seraph: Commonly imperial aligned holy crusaders or members of the secret sect that try to recover lost knowledge of the magic of the valley. 
- Ranger beast master: Bond spirits from the veil to beast to control them, or so rumours say.
- Bards: an ancient art form recovered by the secret sect that try to recover lost knowledge of the magic of the valley. They use music to manipulate the arcane realm. \
