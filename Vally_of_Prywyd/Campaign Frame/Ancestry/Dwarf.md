Seafaring nomads ever since their island home was destroyed by a volcano thousands of years ago, Dwarves are superstitious of land convinced that it lies about its stability, at least the ocean you can tell when it changes. 

1000 years ago Dwarves used magic to keep their volcano at bay, when the metal curse was put in place their magic eventually failed due to the concentration of metal in the earth. Some dwarves saw this coming and built ships in preparation those where the ones who lived.

- Dwarves hand down stones from the beaches of their lost homeland. Pebble beaches. 
- They believe their 'god' betrayed them they are now 
	- Gods are elemental, they speak of the Betrayer Gods Terra and Ignis, and the gods that help them escape Caeruleus and Ventus. 
	- Shamanistic - used to actively worship the elements (GM feel free to adapt). They fear fire and earth elementals (‘betrayed’), see wind and water as protectors/saviours. 
- 850ish years ago Dwarves used magic to keep their volcano at bay, when the metal curse was put in place their magic eventually failed due to the concentration of metal in the earth. Some dwarves saw this coming and built ships in preparation those where the ones who lived.
	- Dwaves before leaving their island home lost their long life spans, this was the first warning sign that their magics, given by the gods, were failing. 
	- Others started making sacrifices to appease their gods
	- The ones that survive found new gods in the sky and sea. abandoning the gods that betrayed them. 

**Cultural beliefs:**
- Avoid stepping onto stone/earth (land is untrustworthy/cursed). 
	- Major trading ports adapted to cater to this and built piers far out from the shore to enable commerce/trade with dwarves. 
	- Most dwarves adhere to this but not all (it isn’t illegal / forbidden, just frowned upon)
- Items from Durmgar’s Vein are considered sacred (from the smallest pebble to intricate jewellery infused with gemstones). Can’t scam a dwarf, they somehow instinctively will know whether the item came from their island / underground (connection to the island elements). 
- Clan loyalty - traditionally family clans were very important. Post the cataclysm, families were broken up so formed new clans around ships. Regularly swapping between ships is seen as suspicious (typically pirate behaviour)
