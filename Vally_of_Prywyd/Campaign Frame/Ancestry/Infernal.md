The amount of people born as inferals has been increasing over the years, people attribute it to many problems, from abandoned children, the disapproval of the gods, and the practice of magic. 

Infernals are unique in their ability to cast magic when in the presence of metal. [[The Empire]] sends out inquisitors to each village to claim infernal children for unknown purposes.

The reason for the increasing appearance of infernals is that [[The Lich]] lord is returning and his magic is reaching out to claim the population as his own. The last time infernals existed was during [[The Lich]] rein, they upheld his Autocracy.
