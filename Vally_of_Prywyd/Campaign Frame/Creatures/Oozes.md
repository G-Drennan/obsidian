
### **Oozes - Diseases**
Oozes are wonderful vectors of Disease, due to their warm liquid bodies. 
#### Ooze parasitic reproduction.
Oozes reproduce asexually but require a warm host as an environment.  
**Stages** 
- The victim's blood thickens into brightly coloured slime and The victim periodically coughs up harmless oozes the size of apples. At this stage the Ooze can be removed with a exact dose of poison. 
- The victim gradually loses rigid body structure and develops unnaturally flexible limbs, + 1 to agility. 
- The victim slows -precises movement becoming more difficult as the body losses structure- and becomes bloated. Something large and gelatinous can be felt shifting inside the victim's torso. This last a until the Ooze leaves the body, ultimately taking the majority of the bodies acid and other liquids, if not managed this can be fatal.
