- Stats Panther with the following abilities
	- 5 Stress, HP pool 5/7, every 4 hp loose a monster to spotlight. 
	- **Relentless 2** - passive: can be spotlighted up to 2 times per spotlight
	- **Mind Dance** - Action: Mark a stress to create a magical dazzling display as the Myst Cat moves thru the mist they created. All targets within close range make **Instinct Reaction Roll** **DC 14**. For each target who failed, gain a fear and the Myst Cat learns one of the targets fear.
	- **Hallucinatory Breath** - Reaction: Countdown 1d6
	  **Countdown (Loop 1d6)**. When the Myst Cat takes damage for the first time, activate the countdown. When it triggers, the Myst Cat **breathes hallucinatory gas on all targets in front of them up to Far range**. Targets must succeed on an **Instinct Reaction Roll** or be tormented by fearful hallucinations. **Targets whose fears are known to the Myst Cat have disadvantage on this roll.** Targets who **fail** must **mark** a **Stress** and lose a **Hope**.
	- Physical attacks from bronze weapons have an extra +d4 damage to the Myst Cat.
- Breaths a fog that hides its approach, the fog is laced by a hallucinogen if the cat is female occasionally.
