1. **Glow Stool**
	- A fungi the glows when living creatures approach
	- This fungi can be used to make viles that glow when a certain creature approaches by diluting it it can detect larger creatures more easily than smaller ones.
2. **Violet Stalk**
	- Grows in tall skinny purple stalks
	- A fungi that shrikes when plucked.
	- Potions made with this one can increase the sound of the users voice dramatically but temporarily,  the after effects is a sore throat and a pitchy voice until the next long rest.
		- checks to sing are made with disadvantage.  
3. **Wizard's Eye.** 
	- Know for their Orangeish glow. The mushroom itself is a small round ball that is Orangeish brown and translucent. 
	- Cooked in stew they are tasty and non-reactive 
	- Combine with a fresh eyeball from any form of humanoid they create a useful item called a Wizard's Eye. 
		- Placing the mushroom and the fresh eye in a jar together triggers the mushroom to extrude small filaments into the eye and begin to absorb it.  
		  Anyone who eats a tendril can see what the new 'Wizard's Eye sees for 2d12 hours.
		- They are often placed near dungeon doors, in trees, and stuck to ceilings with putty. The Thieves Guild has been known to pay 100gp for specimens as they seem to have no shortage of eyes to use them with.
4. **Latch Cap**
	- An invasive mushroom from [[The Kingdom of Dol'vyna]] that can grow anywhere.
	- When touched it releases spores, They take root as soon as they find a surface. In 2d4 weeks mushrooms will grow where they landed. 
	- Eating them cooked is possible, and they have a nutty flavour. Eating them raw is considered poison as the spores get into the air ways of the consumer, where they will grow and stop breathing. 
5.  **Gripa**. 
	- Sometimes known as the sign of tragedy, or the plant of deaths. Rumor has it gripa grows where tragedies have taken place, as a memory of the fallen and their miseries.
	- This blue stalk grows as a product of the decomposition of a humanoid. 
	- Used for blue  dye.
6. **Davian's Tree.** 
	- A hafling-sized brown capped mushroom that retreats underground during the day and emerges at night, appearing to “deflate” or otherwise shrink as it disappears.
	- It’s flesh is a delicacy, and during summer it releases spores that are said to be able to mask the taste of any good or drink.
	- This mushroom is considered a famine food. 
7. **Wolfs Paw** 
	- A fury paw shaped mushroom brought over from [[The Kingdom of Dol'vyna]] where it is actively farmed and turned into potions where its effects are controlled. 
		- Its used by adventures and commoners to keep warm in winter in Dol'Vyna when travelling or fuel for fire runs low. 
		- This mushroom is why rumours of werewolves come from Dol'vyna.
	- Fruits only on full moons. 
	- Consumers grows a thick winter fur coat similar to that of a wolf, the coat can be removed with a shave with a 1/20 chance to grow back.  
8. **Scholars Trick**
	- A fungi that grows in clusters of small square like caps.
	- When consumed in increases **Knowledge**  by  `dice: 1d4` for until the end of the next long rest.
	- Gives the users a feeling that everything has slowed down
9. **Cats Nightcap**
	- A pointed orange mushroom with a black stripe down its center that makes it remincent of a cast eye.
	- The mushroom can be used in potions for sleeping or dark vision based on how its mixed.
10. **Spirits Delight**
	- A blue mushroom that has spores that make a sparkly mist.
	- Consuming the mushroom allows the user to see spirits from the Aethel Realm.
		- **Side effect** insanity. 
	- The spores can be used as an indicator of a spirit presents, often increasing in lumiosity when in close proximity to the spirit reflecting their movements and hinting at their form.
	- This mushroom is grown by the [[The Empire of Ru'Celious]] Veritiors. 
11. **Mozok Stem**
	- A fleshy grey mushroom with pink sap that seeps from its crevices. 
	- Increase knowledge temporarily. 
	- **Side effects:** Head aches, swelling of the cranium, insanity.
