- Farming and logging village.
## Points of interest
### Saw Mill
The loggers mill is four buildings, the mill that cuts the logs, the communal space, the lodgings., and the boat house All these buildings are the oldest in the village but are regularly maintained by the lumberjacks with repairs of varying carpentry skill. 
- **Communal space:** where the lumberjacks cook, eat and do business. 
- **Lodgings:** Where the lumberjacks that come here for seasonal work sleep.
- **Saw Mill:** operated all day to cut logs down into smaller parts to be transported up river.
- **Boat House:** No boats are docked in the boathouse until the [[Intro adventure - Gorgon of Farfield|curse]] in the forest is lifted. 

 - **Orion Oak - Lead Lumberjack:** A strict and ill tempered man hailing from the empire 
	 - He left the empire after his lumber business there failed and he was humiliated. He will not let that happen again, even due to a [[Intro adventure - Gorgon of Farfield|curse]].  
	 - He is the son of an old favoured servant to [[Lord Theodore Hardwyn]]. 
	 - He will be found complaining to his valley workers who are more superstitious than him, "Don has gone for a wonder, when he is back he is as good as fired from my mill."
	   "People don't turn to stone, the place where you showed me his statue would be is not there. He is just lazy." 
### Blacksmith
The Korval' family business, a family of 8 most working age bar the youngest who is tasked with collecting water for the forge. 
- Janna Korval: A smart young girl with an eye for business, She helps her mother with Keeping the smithy inventory and accounting. 
- Raine: A young girl who is egger to wrok the forge but her mother wont allow her to untill she is older, in between fetching water she is watching her family work the forge. 
- Rysan: being the middle child of the family and deft makes it easy for them to be forgotten,. When not working the forge Rysan helps out at the local Imperial Temple.
- Haiden & Hogar Korval: Twins and blacksmith apprentices. The are often competing. 
- Branson Korval: Eldest son, aspires to be a guard for the town ever since the town was attacked by brigands 4 years ago. Due to the amount of work the smithy has received lately his farther has requested his assistance in the forge.
- Bran Korval: The farther, focused on his work man of little words. He has been annoyed lately of the amount of work that has come about because of the cursed forest but he accepts his wife's wisdom in making the most of it while it lasts. 
- Tress Korval: The face of the smithy, she ensures that smithy runs smoothly.
	- "Weapons? We don't receive orders for those often but we can do that."

### The Messengers Trade - General store
- The shop sign shows an image of a young woman and a donkey.
- Inside a vast display of prosthetic hang on the walls.
- **Olga Dunlow - owner:** The stoic shop owner. In her youth she worked as a messenger and scout for the valley during the empires conquest. After an injury severed her left leg she still insisted to work as a messenger, crafting an array of wooden prosthetic to aid her. 
	- Olga movements are short and she will often grimace during.
	- After being convinced Olga will mention that her arthritis from her prolong use of prosthetic is paining her. The Priest in the forest hasn't visited her in a few weeks. 

### Guard keep
Maintains a total of 2 trained guards from the capital. 
- Ferdinand: A grizzled but easy going veteran who is retiring out in this village, he served at the end of the empire conquest of the valley when he was a young man. This village was where he grew up, he hopped to return to his childhood sweet hart but she died to plague.
- Brig: A young brash woman who is convinced she was sent out to this village for punishment after failing to raid a thief guild hideout. In actuality her higher ups sent her out here to learn from the veteran.
In the mornings they offer training to the population of Farfield, boys older than 14 are required to take militias training so they can defend the village. 

**Local problems**
- The guards often collect items of the old religion from around town.
	- Brig wants to report the people who still worship the old religion as heritcs.
	- Ferdinand says reporting them will only displease [[Lord Theodore Hardwyn]] who relies on the village as an income source. 
		- Ferdinand is often trying to help the population hide their religion better. But he population is relaxed on this due to the limited reach of the empire.
