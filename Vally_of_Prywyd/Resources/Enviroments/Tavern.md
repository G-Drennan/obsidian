**Adversaries:** Guards, Mercenaries. 
**Impulses:** Provide opportunities for adventures, nurture community. 
##### Features
- **Rumours - Passive:** Roll *Presences* as 1 on a success 2 on a critical success.
	- A fascinating rumour with a connection to the PC backstory.
	- A promising job for the party involving a nearby threat or situation.
	- Local folklore that relates to something they've seen.
	- Town gossip that hints at a community problem.
	*Who has the information? What gossip do locals start spreading about the PC?*
- **Sing for your supper- Passive:**  Roll *Presence*, earn 1d4 gold on a success 2d4 on a critical. Mark stress on a failure. 
	*What do you perform? What does the piece mean to you? When was the last time you performed for a crowd?*
- **Bar Fight - Action:** ***Spend a fear*** to have a bar fight erupt in the tavern. when a PC tries to move through the tavern while the fight persists, they must make an *Agility* or *Presence* roll or take 1d6+2 physical damage from a wild swing or thrown object. 
		*Who started the fight? What will it take to stop it?*
