**Difficulty:** Tier 1 
**Impulse:** To press inward, to bury those who enter 
**Adversaries:** Cave Fauna, Echo-Bats, Stone-Lurkers

**Features:**

- - **Tight Passage** — **Reaction:**  A narrow passageway is in the way of the PCs. When a character squeezes through, they roll **Agility**; on a fail, the stone constricts and they gain **1 Stress**.
   _“What part of your body brushes the stone?”_
    
- **Everything Looks the Same**  — **Passive:** When navigating or retracing steps, a character rolls Instinct or Knowledge. On a failure, they become turned around losing time.
  _“What detail convinces you you’ve been here before? What makes you doubt it?_
    
- **Unsteady Ground** — **Action:**  **Spend a Fear**  Loose shale shifts violently. When a character moves across unstable terrain, they roll **Agility**. On a failure, they slip and take **1d6 physical damage**. 
  _“The floor gives way beneath you, as if the cave wants to see you fall. What sound echoes through the cavern as you stumble?*
    
- **Deep Cold** — **Reaction** After 6 actions in the cave, each character rolls Fortitude. On a failure, they gain 1 Stress unless they warm themselves.
  _“When was the last time you felt comfortable?”_
    
- **Darkness** —  **Reaction** Torchlight is required to see; each torch lasts **2d12 actions**. When performing an action in torchlight, a character rolls **Agility**; on a failure, their torch flickers out. 
  *“The wind snuffs the flame for a heartbeat, and the dark presses in like a living thing What shape does the darkness take as it closes around you?"*
    
- **Flood** — **Action:**  **Spend a Fear**. Water begins pooling rapidly. Movement becomes difficult: when moving through rising water, a character rolls **Agility**. On a failure, they lose **1 action**. After **2d4 rounds**, the chamber fully floods; characters underwater take **1 Stress each round**.
  _“Water climbs your legs, eager to claim the space you occupy.”_
    
- **Cave Rest** —  **Passive:** Camping here is uneasy. When resting, each character rolls **Instinct**; on a fail, shifting stone or dripping echoes interrupt their rest and they gain **1 Stress**.
   _“What disturbs your sleep?.”_
