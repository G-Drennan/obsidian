- left her [[Dwarf]] ship against tradition. 
- Ship of monks
- Home: [[Wayfarer’s Anvil ]]
	Around 40 or so monks in this crew
	A simple ship (austere / minimalist) in line with humble nomadic monk life
	Make a modest income by offering spiritual services to the dwarven diaspora (and non-dwarves who are curious or see this as a novelty) 
	Blessings for voyages, rituals for safe passage, interpreting omens from tides/storms, mediating between ships
	Most have some training in healing or fighting (particularly the latter since Captain Runa joined the crew)
	Have so far been safe on the seas and avoided pirate raids (typically dwarven pirates know not to mess with the monk ship in fear of primal consequences) 
	
- [[Elder Brumhall:]] Spiritual leader and lorekeeper 
	In his 70s 
	Wears simple green robes and walks with a staff. Is bald but has a thick grey beard tied at the bottom. 
	Light-hearted and humorous but is also wise, thoughtful and strategic (driven by his desire to learn the truth of the volcanic eruption). 
	Mentor figure: 
	Encouraged Gwen to journey to the land: 
	Confident she is well trained and can defend herself. 
	Interested in uncovering the truth of what caused the volcanic eruption and believes exploring the land will help. 
	Thinks dwarves need to move beyond superstitions to discover the truth. Encourages Gwen to ‘find herself’
	Sees Gwen as a potential successor despite her youth / lack of book smarts. 
	
- [[Captain Runa Brinebreaker]]: 
	Is the ‘face’ of the ship to outsiders (e.g. when at human ports getting provisions) and is responsible for logistics / management of the ship.  
	Sees herself as the only practical person on a ship full of monks with their ‘heads in the clouds’. Grumpy but is deep-down fond of the monks and fiercely protective. World-weary. 
	In her 40s. Wears gaudy gemstones and colourful silken shirts (contrast to the rest of the ship) 
	10 years ago she joined Wayfarer’s Anvil from another ship (left for reasons she won’t talk about, only Elder Brumhall knows…perhaps a pirate background…?).
	Perhaps is secretly supplementing the ship’s income with some shady trading on the side… (they’ve all got to eat)
